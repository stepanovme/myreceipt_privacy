# 1. About the App

“My Receipts” is designed to:

* scan receipts;
* recognize information from receipts;
* store receipt photographs and files;
* store structured purchase information;
* organize and search receipts;
* provide access to purchase history;
* allow users to share individual receipts with other users.

To use the App, users create an account using an email address.

---

# 2. Information We Process

## 2.1. Account Information

When you create and use an account, we process:

* your email address;
* a unique account identifier;
* information required for authentication and maintaining your session.

Your email address is used to identify your account, authenticate you, and provide access to your data.

---

## 2.2. Receipt Files and Images

You may take photographs of receipts using your device camera or upload receipt images and files from your device.

The App may store the following in cloud infrastructure:

* photographs of receipts;
* receipt images;
* receipt files uploaded by you;
* original files required to display or reprocess a receipt.

These files are treated as user data and are stored in connection with your account.

---

## 2.3. Recognized Receipt Information

When a receipt is processed, the following information may be stored:

* purchase date and time;
* merchant name;
* store or point-of-sale name;
* product and service names;
* quantities;
* units of measurement;
* prices;
* discounts;
* taxes, where shown on the receipt;
* total purchase amount;
* payment method, where shown on the receipt;
* fiscal information;
* fiscal document number;
* fiscal attribute;
* cash register registration number;
* other information contained in the receipt;
* product categories;
* notes and other information added by the user.

The actual information processed depends on the contents of each receipt.

# 3. Camera and File Uploads

The App requests permission to use your device camera in order to photograph receipts.

Images captured using the camera are sent to the server for recognition and may be stored in cloud storage as receipt files.

You may also upload existing receipt photographs or files from your device.

Access to the camera and photos is provided only after the corresponding permission has been granted by you.

You may change these permissions at any time through your device settings.

---

# 4. Receipt Recognition

Receipt recognition is performed on the server side.

For OCR processing, a receipt image or file is transmitted to the App's server infrastructure.

The server extracts text and structured information from the receipt.

The recognition result is used to create a purchase record and display receipt information in the App.

The original receipt file or photograph may also be stored in cloud storage.

---

# 5. Cloud Storage

User data is stored in cloud infrastructure used to operate the App.

The cloud infrastructure may store:

* account information;
* receipt photographs;
* receipt images;
* uploaded receipt files;
* OCR results;
* structured purchase information;
* receipt history;
* categories and user notes;
* information about shared access;
* technical information required to operate the account.

Data is stored in connection with the user's account and is used to provide the functionality of the App.

---

# 6. Sharing Receipts

The App allows users to share individual receipts with other users.

When a receipt is shared, the recipient may be able to access information contained in that receipt, including:

* the receipt image or file;
* recognized receipt information;
* products;
* prices;
* purchase date;
* merchant information;
* total amount;
* other information contained in the shared receipt.

The user decides who receives access to their receipts.

The user may revoke shared access using the corresponding functionality of the App.

After shared access is revoked, the recipient should no longer be able to access the receipt through the App's sharing mechanism.

Revoking access does not guarantee deletion of information that the recipient may have previously copied, saved, or otherwise obtained while access was available.
